Burrito

Home

Hearty, cheesy bean burritos with seasoned pinto beans, rice, and all the classic fixings — ready in about 30 minutes.

For extra flavor, mash some of the beans as they cook for a creamier texture. Add sautéed peppers, cooked ground beef, or chorizo for a heartier filling. Wrap tightly and pan-sear the burritos seam-side down for a crispy exterior.

Ingredients

  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 2 cups cans pinto or black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoons tbsp olive oil
  • 1 teaspoons cumin
  • 1 teaspoons chili powder
  • 0.5 cups cup water or broth
  • 1.5 cups cups cooked rice
  • 1.5 cups cups shredded cheddar or Mexican blend cheese
  • 0.5 cups cup salsa
  • 0.5 cups cup sour cream
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 0.3 cups cup chopped cilantro
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Steps

  1. Sauté onion and garlic: Heat 1 tablespoons tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add 1 small onion, diced and cook until softened, about 4 minutes.
  2. Cook the beans: Add 2 cups cans pinto or black beans, drained and rinsed, 1 teaspoons cumin, 1 teaspoons chili powder, and 0.5 cups cup water or broth to the skillet. Simmer, stirring occasionally and mashing some beans against the pan, until thickened, about 8 minutes.
  3. Warm the tortillas:Warm 4 large flour tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave until pliable, about 20 seconds each.
  4. Assemble the filling: Lay out each tortilla and layer with bean mixture, 1.5 cups cups cooked rice, and 1.5 cups cups shredded cheddar or Mexican blend cheese down the center.
  5. Add toppings: Top with 0.5 cups cup salsa, 0.5 cups cup sour cream, 1 avocado, sliced, and 0.3 cups cup chopped cilantro as desired.
  6. Roll and serve: Fold in the sides of the tortilla, then roll tightly from the bottom up to seal. Serve with lime wedges.
