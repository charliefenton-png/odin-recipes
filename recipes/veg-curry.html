Vegetable Curry

Home

A cozy, spiced coconut curry loaded with vegetables — great over rice with warm naan on the side.

To make this dish, serve your curry over hot basmati rice or with fresh warm naan bread. Swap the chickpeas for firm tofu or paneer cubes to add more protein. Change the amount of chili flakes up or down to make the food spicier or milder

Ingredients

  • tablespoons vegetable oil
    • medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoons fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tablespoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoons ground turmeric
  • 0.5 teaspoons red chili flakes (optional)
  • 400 grams canned diced tomatoes
  • 400 milliliters canned coconut milk
  • 250 milliliters vegetable broth
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 1 potato, cubed
  • 2 cups cauliflower florets
  • 1 cups green beans, trimmed and halved
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 cups chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 teaspoons salt
  • 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Steps

  1. Sauté aromatics: Heat 2 tablespoons vegetable oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add 1 medium onion, finely chopped and cook until softened, about 6 minutes.
  2. Bloom the spices: Add 2 tablespoons curry powder, 1 teaspoons ground cumin, 1 teaspoons ground turmeric, and 0.5 teaspoons red chili flakes (optional). Stir constantly and cook for about 1 minute.
  3. Build the base: Pour in 400 grams canned diced tomatoes, 400 milliliters canned coconut milk, and 250 milliliters vegetable broth. Stir well and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Add the hearty vegetables: Add 2 carrots, sliced and 1 potato, cubed. Cover and simmer until they begin to soften, about 10 minutes.
  5. Add remaining vegetables: Stir in 2 cups cauliflower florets, 1 cups green beans, trimmed and halved, 1 red bell pepper, sliced, and 1 cups chickpeas, drained and rinsed. Cover and simmer until all vegetables are tender, about 15 minutes.
  6. Finish the curry: Stir in 2 cups baby spinach and 1 teaspoons salt and cook just until the spinach wilts, about 2 minutes.
  7. Serve: Ladle into bowls, garnish with 2 tablespoons fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish, and serve with 1 lime, cut into wedges wedges on the side.
